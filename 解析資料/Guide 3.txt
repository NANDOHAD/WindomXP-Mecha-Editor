Because there are many logical relationships in ani, after conditional judgment or key input, there are many statements that jump from one action to another, and the numbers called by these statements are the corresponding action numbers. The action numbers of all the bodies in the original version are Based on the same logical framework, only the use of more and less of the action, so this is universal. I can say that I am the first person to realize and make a preliminary summary. In the top 100, there are two sets of actions for holding a gun and holding a sword. The one listed at the front is the action with the gun, and the one at the back is the action with the sword.

The easiest way to find the action number through the corresponding tail of the action is to delete a semicolon in the tail. When you open the game and load the body, the game will automatically report an error and prompt that the semicolon error is in the numbered action and in the tail. The approximate location.
